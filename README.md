## Hey, I'm Erfan

I'm a frontend engineer from Mashhad, Iran. I've been writing React and Next.js professionally for about four years, mostly on products that real teams use every day: CRMs, an internal ERP, a couple of e-commerce sites and a university booking system.

Lately I care less about "building websites" and more about building the tools a business actually runs on. If a company is still taking orders over WhatsApp and writing invoices by hand, that's the kind of problem I like to fix. I usually do the UI in Next.js and glue the rest together with Node and n8n.

**Right now**

- Frontend developer at Yekta Group, working on our internal ERP (docs archive, chat, attendance, tasks) in React + shadcn/ui
- Hooking a Hugging Face sentiment model into our customer feedback flow so the sales team doesn't have to read every comment
- Doing my master's in AI at Khayyam University

**A few things I'm proud of**

- Built the Helium Park customer portal and CRM on my own, from an empty repo to production. Support response time dropped about 30% after launch.
- Designed an RFM segmentation model there that helped push customer retention up around 25%.
- At Negah Rahgosha I replaced polling with Socket.io notifications and cut update latency by roughly 40%.
- I try to keep Lighthouse above 90 on anything I ship. It's not always easy with heavy dashboards.
